Sign Language Recognition Models

State-of-the-Art Research for SignBridge System Upgrade

2024-2025 Technical Research Report | Target: ASL Citizen 2,731 Classes

Executive Summary

This research identifies the 7 strongest candidate models for upgrading SignBridge from its current 49.75% accuracy baseline. Models were evaluated against SignBridge's requirements: 2,731-class ASL Citizen dataset, real-time webcam inference, fine-tuning with limited data (15 samples/class), and substantial performance improvement.

Current Baseline 49.75% accuracy
Target Dataset ASL Citizen (2,731 classes)
Real-time Requirement Webcam inference ≤100ms
Data Efficiency 15 samples/class fine-tuning

Critical Integration Factors

Webcam Compatibility

Pose-based models (SPOTER) are most compatible with existing SignBridge MediaPipe pipeline.

Data Efficiency

Transformer-based models with pretraining excel with 15 samples/class for fine-tuning.

Performance Boost

Expected accuracy: 60-85% with proper fine-tuning (vs current 49.75%).

Compute Requirements

Pose-based inference works on CPU, multimodal may need GPU for optimal performance.

Available Resources

Conclusion

The research indicates that transformer-based architectures, particularly those using pose input or multimodal fusion, offer the most promising path forward for SignBridge. The combination of SPOTER's efficiency and SignVLM's large-vocabulary capabilities provides a strong foundation for achieving substantially better performance on the 2,731-class ASL Citizen dataset.

Key recommendations:

  1. Immediately prioritize SPOTER for its compatibility with existing pose-based pipeline
  2. Establish evaluation pipeline for testing model performance on ASL Citizen 2,731 classes
  3. Plan phased integration strategy combining immediate improvements with long-term optimization
  4. Allocate resources for model fine-tuning with limited data (15 samples/class)
