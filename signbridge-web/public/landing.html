05 Questions

Good questions.
Honest answers.

Who is SignBridge for?

For Deaf and hard of hearing people who sign, and for everyone who wants to understand them: family, friends, coworkers, teachers and anyone learning sign language.

Which sign language does it understand?

American Sign Language, also known as ASL.

Do I need any special equipment?

No. A regular webcam is enough. Good light on your hands helps.

Does it speak out loud?

Yes. SignBridge can say your words in a natural voice, or you can keep it silent and let people read the words on screen.

Can it learn my own signs?

Yes. You can teach SignBridge your own signs so it understands the way you sign.

Can I use it to learn ASL?

Yes. Practice letters and words, watch how the hand should move, and see right away when you get it.
